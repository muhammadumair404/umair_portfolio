Vaihok — Private, All-in-One Super App

Meet Vaihok — your private, all‑in‑one super app. Chat securely, make crystal‑clear voice and video calls, pay and get paid, and access everyday services without juggling multiple apps.

Key Features:
  • End-to-End Encrypted Messaging: Secure 1:1 and group chats with robust privacy protection, sharing of photos, videos, files, voice notes, and locations.
  • Crystal-Clear Calls: High-definition voice and video calling optimized for performance and reliability even on slow network connections.
  • Payments & In-App Wallet: Fast payments and requests, secure digital wallet, instant peer-to-peer and merchant payments, and financial account management.
  • Services Marketplace: Integrated ecosystem to order, track, book, and pay for services from trusted partners in one centralized place.
  • Communities & Channels: Public and private channels, community building, and smart notifications.
